---
name: Bibliography versus evidence
description: Why clean academic reference lists must remain separate from page-specific retrieval evidence.
---

List a paper once in a numbered bibliography, even when several PDF pages supplied evidence. Keep retrieval-status and license notes out of the formal reference entry.

**Why:** The user found repeated page-level entries and retrieval notes unprofessional. Those page records still matter for checking quotations and claims; removing them from the evidence store would lose traceability.

**How to apply:** Deduplicate for presentation/export, not by deleting saved evidence. Remap body citations and figure captions together. Preserve original evidence identifiers, excerpts and source quotations. Use real DOI/source hyperlinks, retain missing metadata as missing, and do not invent bibliographic details.
