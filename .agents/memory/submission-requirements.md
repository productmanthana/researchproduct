description: Product expectations for selected-target requirements, research evidence and truthful compliance claims.

The user wants journals and theses to match the requirements of the selected journal or university, with related-paper and patent analysis, possible gaps and improvements in the same workflow. They explicitly chose to build both together.

**Why:** Preset formatting badges were mistaken for actual confirmation. The user challenged how confirmation occurred; a maintained list or saved review date does not establish current compliance.

**How to apply:** Retrieve the selected official instructions, retain their source and retrieval time, ground extracted requirements in real quotations, apply supported layout settings, and expose unmet or manual requirements. Do not call a saved layout verified, infer approval, fabricate signatures/data, or claim all rules were checked when linked documents, departmental variations or unreadable sources remain. Keep originals unchanged when creating formatted or improved copies.

Large catalogue coverage is not the number of papers analysed. Show the actual bounded paper evidence and patent records used, disclose failed patent access, and link overlaps and proposed improvements to retrieved evidence. Related work does not prove commercial availability or absence of novelty.
