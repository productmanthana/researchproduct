description: Product expectations and safeguards for submission-readiness reviews with papers and patents.

Paper Review is a separate research capability, accessible from saved writing, with a downloadable PDF scorecard and project-library history. It combines manuscript feedback with related scholarly work, patent disclosures and candidate research gaps.

**Why:** The user approved a dedicated Paper Review experience after supplying a submission-readiness scorecard example, then explicitly requested paper and patent API comparisons to improve the work and investigate existing related ideas.

**How to apply:** Treat readiness scores as explained AI-assisted rubric estimates, not acceptance probabilities or human peer review. Exclude unassessable categories instead of inventing scores. Proposal reviews must not penalize missing completed experiments. Patent disclosures and scholarly similarities do not establish commercial availability, infringement or absence of novelty; show bounded evidence and candidate gaps. Preserve original manuscripts and require approval before creating revised working versions.
