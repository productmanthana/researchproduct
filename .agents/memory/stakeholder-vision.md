name: Stakeholder vision
description: User-stated ambitions for the Evidora research-to-publication journey.
---
The user wants Evidora presented to researchers and other stakeholders as a natural-language research workflow spanning large scholarly catalogues, evidence-backed literature surveys, originality review before finalising a journal article or thesis, one-click formatting for more than ten journal profiles, and finding potential research gaps from a one- or two-page draft or proposal.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for these five points in a researcher/stakeholder presentation, not merely a generic AI product overview.

**How to apply:** Treat originality review, the ten-plus one-click journal profiles and automated short-proposal gap finding as requested product goals until implemented, not current capabilities. Explain that Q1/Q2/Q3 rank journals rather than define manuscript formats, AI-writing detection is fallible, and catalogue coverage is not a locally stored or fully readable PDF corpus.
