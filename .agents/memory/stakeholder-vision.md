description: User-stated ambitions for the Evidora research-to-publication journey.

The user wants Evidora presented to researchers and other stakeholders as a natural-language research workflow spanning large scholarly catalogues, evidence-backed literature surveys, originality review before finalising a journal article or thesis, one-click formatting for more than ten journal profiles, and finding potential research gaps from a one- or two-page draft or proposal.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for these five points in a researcher/stakeholder presentation, not merely a generic AI product overview.

**How to apply:** Treat originality review, the ten-plus one-click journal profiles and automated short-proposal gap finding as requested product goals until implemented, not current capabilities. Explain that Q1/Q2/Q3 rank journals rather than define manuscript formats, AI-writing detection is fallible, and catalogue coverage is not a locally stored or fully readable PDF corpus.

For the Evidora stakeholder presentation, the user wants detailed, pointwise explanations with separate slides for each capability. Cover plagiarism checking separately from ChatGPT/Claude limitations, and include document knowledge-base upload, document Copilot questions, journal formatting, thesis preparation and one-click export as distinct topics.

**Why:** The user rejected the five-slide version as “too short and not clear” and repeated the need for separate feature explanations.

**How to apply:** Expand the number of slides rather than compressing several capabilities into a brief overview. Keep descriptions of current foundations separate from proposed integrated workflows.

In stakeholder slides, omit provider names such as OpenAlex and explanations about PDFs not being stored. Focus on researcher benefits instead.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removing these details from the presentation.

**How to apply:** Describe scholarly discovery, natural-language questions, deduplication and traceable evidence without infrastructure detail. Keep coverage and access claims accurate without implying every indexed work has accessible full text.
